PROBLEM TITLE

AI-Enabled Real-Time Digital Twin System for Health Monitoring, Fault Prediction and Mission Reliability Enhancement of Aero Piston Engines used in MALE UAVs.

BACKGROUND & OPERATIONAL CRITICALITY

Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) UAVs like the DRDO Tapas-BH-201 (Rustom-II) execute strategic long-duration intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance (ISR), and maritime border patrolling for up to 24+ hours at altitudes between 18,000 and 30,000 ft. The propulsion system (turbocharged aero piston engine) is the single point of failure: an in-flight engine flameout, cylinder seizure, or turbocharger failure results in catastrophic asset loss, mission abort, or uncontrolled crashes into contested territories.

SHORTCOMINGS OF CURRENT SYSTEMS

Traditional UAV avionics rely on simple static threshold alarms (e.g., CHT > 220°C). These are reactive—by the time a static threshold is breached, irreversible mechanical scoring, valve deformation, or catastrophic bearing seizure has already occurred. They lack physics awareness of altitude lapse rates, ambient temperature extremes (-50°C at 25,000 ft), and incipient multi-signal degradation patterns.

OUR PROPOSED INDIGENOUS SOLUTION

  • Physics-Informed Digital Twin (PIDT): Combines 0D/1D thermodynamic Mean Value Engine Models (MVEM) with Deep Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN) running on low-SWaP airborne edge compute (FPGA/Jetson) and GCS.
  • Incipient Anomaly Detection: Predicts micro-faults 45–90 minutes before failure (e.g., lean pre-knock, wastegate leakage, injector clogging, oil cavitation).
  • Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Forecasting: Dynamic Arrhenius wear & Gaussian Process degradation curves with confidence intervals.
  • Mission Contingency Engine: Real-time in-flight safe recovery corridor calculator with autonomous throttle derate advisory to guarantee safe return to base (RTB).
STRATEGIC DEFENCE IMPACT: Eliminates reliance on foreign proprietary diagnostics (Austro/Rotax), supports Atmanirbhar Bharat, cuts UAV fleet lifecycle maintenance costs by 38%, and enhances operational sortie completion rates across Indian Armed Forces.
